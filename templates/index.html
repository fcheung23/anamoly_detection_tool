Notes

Silence Threshold controls how long a sensor must be silent before it gets flagged. Adjust based on observations based on previous activity.

House-wide Window and Sensor Ratio determine when a silence is classified as a home absence vs. an individual sensor malfunction. If most sensors go silent together, it is treated as an absence.

Absent Firing flags sensors that continue firing during a detected home absence — these are the strongest indicators of a sensor malfunction or false trigger.
